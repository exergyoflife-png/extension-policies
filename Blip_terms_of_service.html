1. What Blip provides

Blip helps you summarize and ask questions about supported videos, web pages, documents and spreadsheets. Available features depend on the page, content permissions, your selected AI provider and model, and any optional speech setup.

2. Your providers and API keys

You choose an AI provider and supply your own API key. You are responsible for your provider account, key security, applicable charges and compliance with that provider’s terms. Optional local features may require separate downloads. Blip does not guarantee that any third-party service or model will remain available.

3. Content and responsible use

Use Blip only with content you are permitted to access and process. Respect applicable laws, privacy, intellectual property rights and the terms of the websites you use. Do not use Blip to bypass access restrictions or process someone else’s data without permission.

4. Google Sheets and data handling

Connecting Google Sheets is optional. Google’s read-only permission covers spreadsheets your account can access; Blip uses it for selected sheets you work with and does not edit, create or delete spreadsheets. Relevant source content and conversation context are sent to your chosen AI provider to generate responses. Google access tokens are not sent to AI providers. You can disconnect in Blip’s Google Sheets settings or revoke access in your Google Account.

The Blip privacy policy explains local storage, permissions, AI requests, optional connections and data controls in more detail.

5. AI output and availability

AI responses may be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. Check important answers against the original source. Blip is not a substitute for professional advice. The extension is provided as available, without a promise of uninterrupted operation or accurate results.

6. Updates and ending use

Features and these terms may change. Updated terms will be posted here with a revised date. You can stop using Blip at any time by disconnecting optional integrations and uninstalling the extension. Separately installed speech helpers, downloaded models and data retained by third-party providers may require separate removal, as explained in the privacy policy.
