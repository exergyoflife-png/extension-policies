Read the data policy ↗
Google Sheets connection READ ONLY
Permission with a purpose.
Google’s read-only Sheets permission covers spreadsheets your account can access. Blip uses it to read selected sheets opened in, or linked from, a page where you use Blip. It does not list every spreadsheet or browse your Drive.
Understand without editing.
Blip reads sheet metadata and bounded cell ranges for summaries and answers. It cannot edit, create, or delete your spreadsheets through this connection.
You choose what connects.
Relevant cell content and your question go to your selected AI provider for a response. Google access tokens are not sent to AI providers. Disconnect in Blip’s Google Sheets settings, or revoke access in your Google Account.
PRIVACY, IN PLAIN SIGHT

Your browser.
Your keys.
Your call.

Blip has no developer-operated AI backend and does not collect usage analytics. Your extension settings and chat history are stored locally in your browser. AI requests go directly to the provider you choose using your own API key.
